A Letter For My Guddi 💌

Dear Guddi,

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki ek insaan kisi doosre ki life ko itna special kaise bana sakta hai. Phir tum yaad aati ho… aur answer saamne hota hai. ❤️

Mujhe tumhari sirf cute smile ya pretty face pasand nahi. Mujhe tumhari woh chhoti-chhoti baatein pasand hain jo shayad tum khud notice bhi nahi karti. Tumhara hona, tumhara care karna, tumhara apne way mein mujhe samajhna — ye sab mere liye bahut precious hai.

Main future ke har moment ka promise nahi kar sakta, lekin itna zaroor keh sakta hoon: jab tak hum saath hain, main hamari story ko lightly nahi lunga. Main memories banana, tumhe smile karwana aur ordinary days ko bhi special banana chahta hoon.

Aur haan… agar kabhi duniya bahut noisy lage na, toh bas yaad rakhna — kahin na kahin ek pagal insaan hai jiske liye tum “special” word ka meaning ho. 🫶

Forever yours,
❤️
